Course 3

COURSE 3 · ADVANCED · 8 TOPICS · TUTORIALS

Mathematical & Statistical Methods

The mathematics and statistics the models rest on, each implemented in R.

What you’ll be able to do by the end

  • Use eigenvalues, the SVD and graph Laplacians to analyse the matrices that arise in games and networks.
  • Solve linear and dynamic programmes and compute fixed points numerically.
  • Quantify information with entropy, mutual information and KL divergence.
  • Carry out frequentist inference, regression and nonparametric tests.
  • Fit Bayesian models with conjugate priors and MCMC.
  • Estimate causal effects with DAGs, difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity and synthetic control.
  • Model time series with ARIMA, VAR and GARCH, and analyse random graphs, centrality and communities.

Who should take this course

For readers who want the methods behind the models. Courses 1 and 2 supply the examples but are not required; comfort with R and algebra is.
